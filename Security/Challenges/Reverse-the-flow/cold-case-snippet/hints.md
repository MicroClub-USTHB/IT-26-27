# Hints

1. **Hint 1:** Regular search engines rarely index the body text of early 20th-century print articles. Look for a digitized U.S. historical newspaper archive (such as the Library of Congress's *Chronicling America* collection) and query distinctive unredacted phrases from the clipping.
2. **Hint 2:** Once you locate the original issue, inspect the publication's "About" or catalog metadata page—not just the scanned image—to find its standardized catalog identifier (LCCN) and primary title.
